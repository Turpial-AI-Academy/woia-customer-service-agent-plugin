# Method and qualification


This portable root supplies instructions and a deterministic plan guard. Communications, Scheduling, Identity, Knowledge, Documents and business providers remain capability owners resolved through Core resources, not new hard dependencies. Only Core >=0.5.7 is hard. Exact qualified release baseline is declared in dev.woia/manifest.json; a binding is not runtime qualification.

Synthetic negative tests prove helper rejection of forged departments, cross-organization recipients, absent exact authority, stale evidence, takeover races and UNKNOWN retries. They do not prove real ingress/send, 24/7 host activation, lock durability, idempotency-store atomicity, provider notification suppression or business/operator E2E. Qualified adapter authentication, source resolution, durable claims and observation remain organization-host gates. Operator E2E=NOT_RUN; Production Ready=false.

Qualified domain providers retain their typed entities, lifecycle schemas and domain/E2E resources. Customer Service consumes their accepted references and minimum authorized results. Organization owners configure policies, schedules, escalation budgets and consent; no universal values are selected. Missing configuration or unavailable providers returns an owned blocker.
